# Hummus

## Ingredients
- chickpeas
- tahini (sesame paste)
- 1 small onion
- 1 lemon
- salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder

## Instructions
- boil the chickpeas, removing the foam all the while
- put the boiled chickpeas, tahini, diced onion, lemon, and pressed garlic in the mixer
- mix until creamy
- garnish with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of cumin powder
